shatter or fall) · ᵗ typo set (文字PV typography) · ᵏ kinetic set (word by word, beat-locked) · ʰ horror set (needs `"horror": true`)
· ʷ 和風 motif (off with `"wa": false`). Generated from `examples/graphics/lyric-motion.html`; regenerate after `vendor.py`.

in `"cut": {"<n>": {...}}`, or in `"enabled"` to switch parts off. `vos lyrics plan` shows what each cut got.
